The cross-match rule now arrives in three escalating steps instead of all at once.

• CROSS-MATCH — two symbols trade places
• DOUBLE CROSS — both pairs swapped, nothing hits its own
• TRIPLE CROSS — three symbols rotate

The top-of-screen legend now shows only what changed, the field clears at every rule
change, and the new rule holds on screen longer so you can prepare for it.

Also: Remove Ads moved to the title screen, and shared scores now link to the App Store.
