# Privacy Policy — Quadshot

_Last updated: 2026-06-14_

Quadshot ("the app") is a single-player arcade game published by bySubotic.
Your privacy matters, and the app is designed to need none of your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Quadshot does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and
makes no network requests.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data **locally on your device only** (via the
operating system's preferences / local storage). It never leaves the device:

- Your best score and local high-score table
- Game statistics (games played, total hits, best combo, highest phase reached)
- Settings (sound on/off, haptics on/off)
- A flag recording that you have seen the tutorial

You can erase all of this at any time via **Settings → Reset Progress**, or by
uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

Quadshot requests no special permissions. It uses the device's vibration motor
for haptic feedback, which you can disable in Settings.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app.
