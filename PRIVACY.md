# Privacy Policy — Quadshot

_Last updated: 8 July 2026 · applies to Quadshot v1.1.0 and later._
_Canonical, published version: <https://dsuboticgreco.github.io/quadshot/privacy.html>_

Quadshot is a single-player arcade game published by bySubotic (Djordje Subotic).

> **Versions 1.0 and earlier were fully offline and collected nothing.** From
> v1.1.0 the app shows ads, which means a third party — Google — receives data
> from your device.

## What we collect ourselves

**Nothing.** No servers, no accounts, no analytics. We never receive your data.

## What our advertising partner collects

Ads are served by the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)**. The table below is taken
verbatim from Google's own `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` inside the SDK we ship
(`Google-Mobile-Ads-SDK 11.3.0`):

| Data | Linked to you | Used to track you | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device identifier (IDFA / advertising ID) | Yes | **Yes, only if you allow it** | Ad personalisation, measurement |
| Advertising data (ads shown, taps) | Yes | No | Ad delivery, measurement |
| Product interaction | Yes | No | Ad delivery, analytics |
| Coarse location (approximate, from IP) | Yes | No | Region-appropriate ads |
| Performance & other diagnostic data | No | No | SDK reliability |
| Crash data | No | No | Analytics |

No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data. No user
accounts and no user-generated content.

## Tracking, and how to refuse it

On first launch iOS asks whether Quadshot may track you across other companies'
apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency).

- **"Ask App Not to Track"** (or no answer) → the advertising identifier is not
  used for tracking and Google serves **non-personalised ads**. The game is
  identical.
- **"Allow"** → Google may personalise and measure ads using your advertising
  identifier.

Change it any time: _iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking_.

## EEA, UK and Switzerland

Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) is
shown. If consent is required and not granted, **no ads are requested at all** for
that session. The form can be reopened from in-game Settings.

## Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed entirely by **Apple**. We never see
your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app stores one flag on-device
(`quadshot.adsRemoved`). Use _Restore purchases_ in Settings on a new device.

## Stored on your device only

Best score, local high-score table, game statistics, settings, and the
"ads removed" flag. Deleted when you delete the app.

## Children

Rated 4+, but not directed to children and not in the Kids Category. We do not
knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Your rights

We hold no data about you. For data Google holds, see
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset
your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
